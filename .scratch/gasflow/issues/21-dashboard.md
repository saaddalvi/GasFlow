# 21: Dashboard

**What to build:** the first screen shows today's activity and where every cylinder is. Follows the blueprint's dashboard layout, without anything from Insights. Covers user stories 71, 72 and 78.

**Blocked by:** 13: Add cylinders and write-offs; 15: Find bills; 16: Overdue and pending returns.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Today shows "Dispatched today" (with comparison to the same weekday last week, customer count, gas breakdown, 7-day strip and month to date against last month), received from customers, net out, bills entered with the last entry time, and refiller movements
- [ ] Cylinder cards show the total, shop stock by fill state, with customers and at refillers
- [ ] A pending returns card shows total pending cylinders, number of customers and how many are overdue, and links to the list
- [ ] A counts below zero card shows how many are at the shop and how many are with parties
- [ ] Quick actions include New bill, Receive empties, Bills, Stock, Parties, Day ledger, Balance register and, for admins, Opening stock
- [ ] Figures link to the matching filtered list
- [ ] Opening stock is not counted as today's movement
- [ ] Labels use glossary terms, and the page works on a phone
